# Scraply Intake V2 pilot baseline

Use this worksheet with real Scraply work only. Do not enter demo prompts or estimated outcomes.

## Recording rules

- Start the intake timer when the task is created.
- Stop it when the brief is ready to run.
- Count only text entered during intake and brief review.
- Record every field changed during review.
- Mark a critical miss only when it could materially change research or the final decision.
- Record usefulness and regret immediately after reviewing the output.
- Copy runtime and cost from the completed run.
