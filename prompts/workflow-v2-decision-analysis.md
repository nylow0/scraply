Describe material positive and negative consequences. Identify decisive risks with qualitative reasons.
Propose responses with their cost and failure conditions. A proposed response is not tested evidence and does not erase the underlying risk.
Use supplied earlier experiment observations to avoid repeating an ineffective response. Preserve what the user actually reported, including uncertainty; a decision alone is not an observed result.
End with the cheapest useful experiment that has observable pass and fail criteria.
Do not score or rank the option and do not choose for the user.
