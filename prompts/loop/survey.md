# Survey what exists

The evidence holds the pages found by the survey searches, each with a source ID such as S3.

- **priorArt:** what already exists for this seed: products and their prices, published studies and their findings, open datasets, earlier attempts and why they stopped. Say what each one leaves unsolved, if anything. If the seed is already solved well, say that plainly. A few short paragraphs, naming the sources by ID.
- **quotes:** exact sentences from the evidence that later claims about what exists will need: a product stating what it does or costs, a study stating its result, a dataset stating what it covers. Copy each sentence exactly as it appears; one that is not word for word in its source is discarded. Give the source ID, who said or published it, and its kind (vendor for a seller about its product, measured for a study or dataset result, firsthand for someone's own experience, other otherwise).
