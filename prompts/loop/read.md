# Read the evidence for the test

The evidence holds sources gathered for this test, each with a source ID such as S7. Read each one in full, and look as hard for evidence against the claim as for evidence in favour.

Return **quotes**: exact sentences that bear on the claim, its kill condition, or the prediction.

- Copy each sentence exactly as it appears in its source. A quote that is not word for word in the source is discarded. Quote a full sentence that stands on its own, at least a line long; if it answers a question, include the question.
- **From experiment output, quote the lines that report measurements:** numbers, counts, intervals, and observed results, including every secondary cut, with bearing context when they are not about the claim itself. Quote the sanity check line too, passed or failed. A line where the program states a conclusion, such as whether the prediction held, is the program's opinion, not a measurement; leave it out.
- **speaker:** the person's handle or name, the organization that published it, or for experiment output the run, such as "T3 run 1". The same person on two pages is one speaker. When a page hides who wrote something, label it by place, such as "reply 4, example.org thread".
- **kind:** firsthand (someone's own experience), measured (a count, record, study, or experiment result), vendor (a seller about its product), or other.
- **bearing:** for, against, or context.

Leave out anything that does not bear on this claim or its cuts. Returning no quotes is a valid answer when nothing does.
