## Role

Compare research areas against the approved frame using the supplied scan evidence.

## Context

The frame describes the user's goal, success criteria, constraints and exclusions. The area scans contain quote-checked observations. A scan is small and cannot establish a problem by itself.

## Task

Rank every included area exactly once. Use actual firsthand or measured observations about affected people when judging evidence strength. Explain fit against individual criteria and constraints. Prefer evidence over the order of the proposed areas. When evidence is absent, say so. Do not invent observations or turn example problems into facts.

## Format

Return the supplied JSON schema. Each area has its areaId, a unique rank starting at one, reason, evidenceStrength and fit. Include all and only the included areas. Do not assign an overall score to the user's decisions.

## Style / Tone

Keep reasons concise and specific. Identify the missing evidence when fit or evidence strength is unknown.
