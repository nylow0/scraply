Plan search queries for the supplied scope and harvest mode.
Produce 6–8 queries for standard depth; use the requested depth when it differs.
The scope's domain field is a flexible starting context. It may describe a goal, competition, topic, audience, market, rough idea, or a specific situation.
Domain mode investigates that context through neutral queries, including how it works, its constraints, criteria, and surrounding evidence.
Audience mode searches for what the supplied audience does, experiences, and complains about. When no audience is supplied, infer relevant people or groups from the starting context and investigate their experiences without inventing facts.
Make every query cover a distinct part of the scope.
